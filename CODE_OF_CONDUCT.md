# Community code of conduct

Osverse contributors and maintainers are expected to keep project spaces respectful, technically honest, and welcoming.

- Discuss ideas and code, not personal characteristics.
- Give specific, actionable feedback and assume good faith while evidence is being gathered.
- Do not publish another person's private data, credentials, or security report.
- Do not use project spaces for harassment, threats, discrimination, spam, or sustained disruption.
- Respect maintainers' decisions about scope, safety, release readiness, and coordinated disclosure.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when these expectations are violated. Serious conduct concerns should be sent privately through the repository's security advisory channel rather than argued in a public issue.
